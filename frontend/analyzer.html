Research Tool Only
All predictions are based on historical price patterns and statistical models. They do NOT constitute financial advice. Past performance ≠ future results.
Quick Scrips:
Choose a holding to analyse
Pick one of your holdings above, or type any NSE symbol, then run the analysis. It scores two years of daily prices with five models and shows how each one has performed out of sample.
Stop and target odds

Set a stop and a target for buying the selected stock. 10,000 replays built from its own recent trading days, and the same plan started on every past day in its history, show how often the target came first, judged against break-even after charges.

Price alerts

Each alert rings once in the notification bell when the price gets there. Alerts never place orders.

Research committee

Six analysts, a bull and bear debate and a portfolio manager (Claude) review the selected stock using only its price history, your position and stored fundamentals. Every number they cite is checked against that data. The result is text to read: the committee cannot place or change orders.
