<title>Create Database Migrations 019, 020, and 021</title>
<action>
1. Author `db/migrations/019_signal_snapshots.sql`:
   - Table `signal_snapshots` (`id`, `snapshot_date`, `user_id`, `tradingsymbol`, `model_version`, `current_price`, `benchmark_price`, `composite_score`, `signal_label`, `status`, `indicators` JSONB, `monte_carlo` JSONB, `return_5d_stock`, `return_5d_benchmark`, `excess_return_5d`, `realized_5d_at`, `return_20d_stock`, `return_20d_benchmark`, `excess_return_20d`, `realized_20d_at`, `return_60d_stock`, `return_60d_benchmark`, `excess_return_60d`, `realized_60d_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`).
   - Unique constraint on `(user_id, tradingsymbol, snapshot_date, model_version)`.
   - Check constraints for status (`PROVEN_EDGE`, `UNPROVEN_NOISE`, `PENDING`), composite score `[0, 100]`, price `> 0`.
   - Indexes on `(user_id, tradingsymbol, snapshot_date DESC)`, `status`, and GIN on `indicators`.
2. Author `db/migrations/020_backtest_runs.sql`:
   - Table `backtest_runs` (`id`, `run_id`, `tradingsymbol`, `model_version`, `train_start_date`, `train_end_date`, `test_start_date`, `test_end_date`, `train_window_days`, `test_window_days`, `total_folds`, strategy performance metrics, baseline metrics, excess CAGR, cost drag, status, passed_hurdle, hurdle_details JSONB, `created_at`).
   - Table `indicator_evaluations` (`id`, `backtest_run_id` FK, `indicator_name`, in-sample/out-sample IC and p-values, `mean_ic`, `std_ic`, `information_ratio`, `weight`, `is_pruned`, `prune_reason`, `created_at`).
   - Unique constraints and indexes.
3. Author `db/migrations/021_historical_daily_bars.sql`:
   - Table `historical_daily_bars` (`tradingsymbol`, `bar_date`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`, `created_at`).
   - Primary key on `(tradingsymbol, bar_date)`.
</action>
</task>

<action>

5. `HoldingSignalDTO`: Replaces legacy `HoldingPrediction`, containing symbol, prices, composite score, signal label, evidence status, evidence badge, indicators, calibrated Monte Carlo, and backtest summary.
