<objective>
Implement calibrated fat-tailed Monte Carlo simulation with empirical coverage validation:
1. Replace uncalibrated normal distribution geometric Brownian motion with a 3-parameter Student's $t$ distribution to model tail risks and kurtosis in Indian equity delivery.
2. Fit degrees of freedom ($\nu$), location ($\mu$), and scale ($s$) on historical log returns using maximum likelihood estimation (`scipy.stats.t.fit`).
3. Apply empirical coverage calibration: scale prediction cones by comparing theoretical critical values against empirical residual percentiles to ensure central 80% and 95% cones contain realized prices ~80% and ~95% of the time.
4. Banish misleading point forecasts ("Target Price") in favor of probabilistic dispersion percentiles (P10, P25, P50, P75, P90).
5. Author unit and calibration verification tests in `tests/test_monte_carlo.py`.

Output: `src/analytics/calibrated_monte_carlo.py` and passing test suite.
</objective>

<title>Implement Student's t Fitting & Calibration in src/analytics/calibrated_monte_carlo.py</title>
<action>
1. Author `src/analytics/calibrated_monte_carlo.py`:
   - `fit_student_t_parameters(log_returns: np.ndarray) -> tuple[float, float, float]`:
     - Fits $\nu, \mu, s$ using `scipy.stats.t.fit(log_returns)`.
     - Bounds degrees of freedom: $\nu \in [2.5, 30.0]$ for numerical stability.
   - `calibrate_empirical_coverage(log_returns: np.ndarray, df: float, loc: float, scale: float, target_coverage: float = 0.80) -> tuple[float, float]`:
     - Computes theoretical quantile $q_{\text{theo}} = t_{\nu}^{-1}((1 + \text{target})/2)$.
     - Computes empirical standardized absolute residuals $e_t = \frac{|r_t - \mu|}{s}$.
     - Finds empirical quantile $q_{\text{emp}} = \text{Percentile}_{100 \times \text{target}}(e_t)$.
     - Calculates scale multiplier $c = \max(1.0, q_{\text{emp}} / q_{\text{theo}})$.
     - Returns calibrated scale and empirical coverage percentage.
</action>
</task>
