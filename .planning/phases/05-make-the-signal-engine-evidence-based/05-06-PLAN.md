<task id="05-06-01">
<title>Implement Evidence Hurdle Classifier</title>
<action>
1. In `src/analytics/rebalancer.py` (or a helper module `src/analytics/evidence_hurdle.py` imported into rebalancer):
   - `evaluate_evidence_hurdles(backtest: BacktestRunDTO | None) -> tuple[str, str, dict[str, bool]]`:
     - Checks Hurdle 1: Predictive Power ($\overline{\text{IC}} > 0$ and $p < 0.05$).
     - Checks Hurdle 2: Stock Timing Skill ($\text{CAGR}_{\text{strategy, net}} > \text{CAGR}_{\text{stock}}$).
     - Checks Hurdle 3: Market Excess Alpha ($\text{CAGR}_{\text{strategy, net}} > \text{CAGR}_{\text{benchmark}}$).
     - Checks Hurdle 4: Trade Quality (Win Rate $\ge 50\%$, Profit Factor $> 1.0$, Trades $\ge 5$).
     - Returns status (`PROVEN_EDGE` or `UNPROVEN_NOISE` or `PENDING`), badge string, and boolean check dictionary.
</action>
</task>
