<task name="task_1" type="execute">
Upgrade `src/analytics/tax_guard.py`.
- `select_tax_optimized_lots(holding_id: int, target_quantity: int) -> list[LotAllocation]`:
  - Fetches lots from `holding_tax_lots`.
  - Classifies lots into 5 tiers:
    1. Short-Term Capital Loss (`STCL`, highest % loss first).
    2. Long-Term Capital Loss (`LTCL`).
    3. Long-Term Capital Gain (`LTCG`, taxed at 12.5%).
    4. Short-Term Capital Gain (`STCG`, `days_to_ltcg > 30`).
    5. Near-LTCG Deferral (`STCG`, `days_to_ltcg <= 30`): Excluded unless liquidation required.
  - Fills target quantity following this priority.
- `get_annual_tax_harvesting_summary(user_id: str = "default") -> TaxHarvestingSummaryDTO`:
  - Determines current Indian Financial Year (e.g. FY 2025-26 from April 1 to March 31).
  - Queries `get_realized_ltcg_ytd(user_id, fy_start_date)`.
  - Computes `ltcg_exemption_remaining = max(0, 125000 - ltcg_realized_ytd)`.
  - Identifies unrealized STCL lots as `LOSS_HARVEST` opportunities.
  - If currently in Q4 (January 1 - March 31) and `ltcg_exemption_remaining > 0`, scans LTCG lots in profit and proposes `GAIN_HARVEST` up to the remaining exemption.
</task>
